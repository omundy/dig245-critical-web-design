## 👉 Explore the Critical Web Design Index

**Individual —** Browse the [Critical Web Design Index](https://criticalwebdesign.github.io/index/). Pick 2 examples you find surprising or compelling. Write: What makes each work stand out? What design choices make it effective? 

**Group — What makes design "critical"?**
Partners, introduce yourself and share examples. Debrief: What do these pieces have in common that separates them from ordinary design? Build a working definition together. Share in class and return to it at the end of the semester.
