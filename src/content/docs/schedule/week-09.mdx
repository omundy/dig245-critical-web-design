### 9-2 Usability Test Critique

**Learning objectives:** By the end of this session you will be able to:
1. Use the 5-second test to assess whether your design's hierarchy matches your intended hierarchy.
2. Calculate or estimate a SUS score for your own design and identify 3 specific improvements it suggests.
3. Articulate the difference between a design that looks good to you and one that functions well for a first-time user.

**Group — Eye Movement Test Critique**
Show your design for 5 seconds, then hide it. Ask the class: What did you look at first? What do you remember? Does that match your intended hierarchy? Revise based on results.

**Individual — SUS Score Reflection**
Calculate your [SUS score](https://bit.ly/cwd-codepen-sus-prototype) if you ran a test, or self-evaluate: what score would you give your design honestly and why? Write 3 sentences on what you'd change.

### 9-3 Off the Grid — Context Discussion

**Learning objectives:** By the end of this session you will be able to:
1. Identify a historical or contemporary example where breaking design convention was the right critical or communicative choice.
2. Generate 25 quick layout sketches under 5 different constraints using the 5-in-5 method to separate ideation from evaluation.
3. Articulate who benefits from design conventions like the grid, and who decides what "correct" layout looks like.

📌 **Usability Test final due today** — submit final GitHub live link to Moodle before class

📖 **Read before class:** Ch 6 Context, pp. 185–194

**Group — "Off the Grid" Provocation**
Discussion: What does it mean to design something that deliberately breaks convention? Who benefits from grids? Who decides what "correct" layout looks like? Find one example from design history where breaking convention was the right move.

**Individual — 5-in-5 Brainstorm Sprint**
5 minutes × 5 rounds, each with a new constraint — (1) mobile only, (2) no images, (3) no color, (4) no navigation, (5) one element only. Generate 5 thumbnail layouts per round. 25 thumbnails total.
