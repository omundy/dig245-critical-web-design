## Share Prototypes in class

1. Form groups of 3-4 with people working on different projects.
1. **Presenter (3 min):** Demo your prototype live — what works, what's still rough, and one specific question or unknown (concept, design, or tech) you want input on.
1. **Group (2 min):** Respond to the presenter's question first, then add any other feedback.
1. Rotate until everyone in the group has presented.
1. Write down the most useful piece of feedback you got. Post it in Slack and use it to prioritize what to build next.

:::caution
Keep each round to 5 minutes so everyone gets a turn and we have time to work in class.
:::
