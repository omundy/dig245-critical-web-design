### Learning objectives

{/* By the end of this session you will be able to: */}

1. Map the power relationships and economic flows of a digital platform (ownership, payment, profit, exclusion).
2. Apply vocabulary from Ch 1 Context to describe the political economy of a specific web infrastructure.
3. Articulate a critical position on how network infrastructure shapes who has power on the web.




## 👉 Networks discussion

- https://theyrule.net


**Group — Network Power Map**
Small groups (2-3) choose one digital platform (e.g. TikTok, Amazon, Google Maps, Spotify). Map it on paper: Who owns the infrastructure? Who pays? Who profits? Who is excluded? Present in 2 minutes.

**Individual — Critical Reflection (write in class)**
100 words: What is the political economy of the web infrastructure you use daily? Reference at least one idea from the Ch 1 context. 

1. **Photograph** your Network Power Map diagram — label who owns, pays, profits, and who is excluded. Make a clear photo to show the details of your work. 
1. **Update your reflection** text and add the photo on Moodle by midnight.
