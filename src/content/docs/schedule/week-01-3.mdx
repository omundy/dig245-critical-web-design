### Learning objectives

{/* By the end of this session you will be able to: */}

1. Map the power relationships and economic flows of a digital platform (ownership, payment, profit, exclusion).
2. Apply vocabulary from Ch 1 Context to describe the political economy of a specific web infrastructure.
3. Articulate a critical position on how network infrastructure shapes who has power on the web.

📖 **Read before class:** Ch 1 Context, pp. 19–27

**Group — Network Power Map**
Small groups (3–4) choose one digital platform (TikTok, Amazon, Google Maps, Spotify). Map it on paper: Who owns the infrastructure? Who pays? Who profits? Who is excluded? Present in 2 minutes.

**Individual — Critical Reflection (write in class)**
100 words: What is the political economy of the web infrastructure you use daily? Reference at least one idea from the Ch 1 context. Post to Moodle before midnight.
