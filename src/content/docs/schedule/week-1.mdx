### Mon - Course Introduction
🛠 **Work during class:** Module 0.1 *How This Book Is Organized* (pp. 6–10), Module 0.3 *Conventions & Resources* (pp. 16–18)

**Individual — CWD Index**
Browse the [Critical Web Design Index](https://criticalwebdesign.github.io/index/). Pick 2 examples you find surprising or compelling. Write: What's the critical intent? What design choices make it effective?

**Group — What makes design "critical"?**
Table groups share their examples. Debrief: What do these pieces have in common that separates them from ordinary design? Build a working definition together. Return to it at the end of the semester.


---

### Wed - Technical Setup
🛠 **Work during class:** Module 0.2 *Technical Requirements* (pp. 11–15)

**Individual — Setup Checklist**
Follow Module 0.2 to set up your environment: GitHub account, Codespaces, Codepen, course tools. Document every step you get stuck on — this becomes your troubleshooting log for the semester.

**Pair — Peer troubleshooter**
Find one person who got something working that you didn't, and one person you can help. Spend 15 min solving each other's setup issues. Report unresolved blockers to the class.

> 📖 **Before next class (Fri):** Read Ch 1 Context, pp. 19–27

---

### Fri - Ch 1 Context Discussion
📖 **Read before class:** Ch 1 Context, pp. 19–27

**Group — Network Power Map**
Small groups (3–4) choose one digital platform (TikTok, Amazon, Google Maps, Spotify). Map it on paper: Who owns the infrastructure? Who pays? Who profits? Who is excluded? Present in 2 minutes.

**Individual — Critical Reflection (write in class)**
100 words: What is the political economy of the web infrastructure you use daily? Reference at least one idea from the Ch 1 context. Post to Moodle before midnight.
