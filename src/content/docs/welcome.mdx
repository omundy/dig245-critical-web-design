## FAQ


###### Do I need to know how to program? 
No. You will learn to use both markup and programming languages in this class, but experience coding is not a prerequisite.

###### Should I pay for Codecademy? 
Don’t pay for codecademy (unless you want to)

###### When is VAC 212 open? 
VAC 212 is open to currently enrolled ART and FMD students 7 days a week at all hours except 1–6 am. During open hours, your Cat Card should give you access to the building and the lab. See the lab’s class schedule and more info at http://vac212.digitaldavidson.net/


###### Do you use Moodle? 
Yes, to collect links to your work for grading. I will open the course Moodle site in the first week.


###### I need a password for a link in the course. 
See the sidebar on Moodle


###### Should I send emails or use Slack? 
For general questions about the course, assignments, etc. that aren't answered in this document, please post in #general or current semester Slack channel. Specific questions can be sent through DM.
