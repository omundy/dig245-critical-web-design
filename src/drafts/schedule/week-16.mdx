### 16-1 Final Presentations

### Learning objectives

{/* By the end of this session you will be able to: */}
1. Present your final project in 3 minutes — show the work, explain the critical intent, and articulate what the design choices do.
2. Give a "Critical Friend" question to a peer that probes their critical intent deeper rather than evaluating surface execution.
3. Reflect in writing on what changed in how you see, make, and evaluate design work over the course of the semester.

**Group — Full Class Critique**
Each student presents their final project: 3 minutes to show and explain, 2 minutes for class feedback. Class uses "Critical Friend" protocol: one question that probes the critical intent deeper.

**Individual — Course Reflection (written)**
One page: What changed in how you see the web? What will you do differently as a designer, viewer, or user? Reference at least 3 specific projects or readings from the semester. Due by midnight.
