### 4-2 Critical Design — Context Discussion

### Learning objectives

{/* By the end of this session you will be able to: */}

1. Analyze a work of critical design by identifying its subject, critical intent, and the specific design choices that make the critique visible.
2. Teach peers about a CWD Index example using the jigsaw method — researching independently, then explaining clearly.
3. Draft a critical intent statement that names the specific power dynamics or assumptions your Unbranding project examines.

📚 **Read before class:** Ch 3 Context (83–90)

**Group — Critical Design Jigsaw**
Groups of 4, each person assigned one [CWD Index](https://criticalwebdesign.github.io/index/) piece: [Binky](https://criticalwebdesign.github.io/index/#binky), [abstractbrowsing.net](https://criticalwebdesign.github.io/index/#abstractbrowsing.net), [Safebook](https://criticalwebdesign.github.io/index/#safebook), [Do It](https://criticalwebdesign.github.io/index/#do-it). Research 10 min, then teach the group: What is it? What's the critical intent? What design choices make the critique visible?

**Individual — Critical Intent Statement**
Write 100–150 words: What is the critical intent of your [Unbranding](https://github.com/omundy/dig245-unbranding) project? What assumptions or power dynamics in visual branding are you examining? This is your artist statement backbone.

> 🛠 **Next class:** Module 3.1 (91–98) · 📌 Start Unbranding

### 4-3 Design with Figma

### Learning objectives

{/* By the end of this session you will be able to: */}

1. Build a moodboard in Figma that communicates a coherent visual world through curated images, a color palette, and two typeface choices.
2. Write a one-sentence rationale for each typeface choice that connects typographic form to the project's conceptual intent.
3. Evaluate a peer's moodboard for visual voice clarity, critical tension, and one specific revision suggestion.

🛠 **Work during class:** Module 3.1 _Design with Figma_ (91–98)

**Individual — Unbranding Moodboard in Figma**
Following Module 3.1: 12 curated images defining your counter-brand's visual world, a color palette (primary, secondary, accent), 2 typeface choices with a one-sentence rationale each.

**Pair — Moodboard Feedback**
Swap Figma files. Give feedback on: (1) Does the moodboard have a clear visual voice? (2) Is there tension that makes it feel _critical_ rather than just aesthetic? (3) One image you'd swap and why.
